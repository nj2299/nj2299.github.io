N8NHome

A personal, single-user home automation application.

What this is

N8NHome is a private automation setup operated by one individual for their own household. It connects the owner's self-hosted n8n instance to the owner's own Google account so that home-network monitoring results — device lists, connection quality, and firewall summaries from the owner's home router — can be recorded in the owner's Google Sheets spreadsheets and reviewed by the owner.

Who can use it

N8NHome is not a public service. It is not distributed, offered, or sold to anyone. The only authorized user is the application's owner, signing in with their own Google account. There is no sign-up and no third-party access.

What it connects to

With the owner's permission, the application reads and writes spreadsheets that belong to the owner, for the sole purpose of storing and reviewing the owner's own home-network monitoring logs. Details are in the Privacy Policy.
