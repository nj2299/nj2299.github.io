Privacy Policy

N8NHome — effective October 30, 2026

Overview

N8NHome is a personal application operated by a single individual for their own home automation. It has exactly one user: its owner. This policy describes what information the application accesses and how it is handled.

Information accessed

Through Google OAuth, and only with the owner's consent, the application accesses:

  • Google Sheets spreadsheets owned by the user — the application reads and appends rows in the owner's home-network monitoring spreadsheets (device inventories, connection statistics, event summaries, and task lists generated by equipment in the owner's home).
  • Basic account identity — used by Google to complete the sign-in flow.

The application does not access email, contacts, calendars, photos, or files outside the spreadsheets used for the monitoring logs described above.

How information is used

Data is used solely to operate the owner's home automation: recording monitoring results, detecting changes on the owner's home network, and presenting summaries back to the owner. Data remains in the owner's own Google account and on the owner's own home server.

Sharing

No data is sold, rented, shared, or disclosed to any third party. No data is used for advertising. No other person has access to the application.

Retention and revocation

Monitoring records are retained in the owner's spreadsheets under the owner's control and can be deleted by the owner at any time. The owner can revoke the application's access at any time from their Google Account's security settings (Third-party access), which immediately ends all access.
